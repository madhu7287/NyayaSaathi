About the Ministry

Objectives

Objectives of Nyayasaathi Portal

  • Creation of a centralized database of all unorganized workers (UWs) seeded with Aadhaar.
  • Improvement of implementation efficiency of the social security services.
  • Integration of Social Security Schemes managed by MoLE and other ministries.
  • Sharing of worker information with various stakeholders through APIs.
  • Ensuring portability of social security and welfare benefits for migrant workers.
  • Tackling National Crises like COVID-19 by providing a comprehensive database.

Who can register?

  • An unorganised worker (UW).
  • Age between 16-59 years.
  • Not a member of EPFO/ESIC or NPS (Govt. funded).

Who is an Unorganised Worker?

Any worker who is a home-based worker, self-employed worker, or a wage worker in the unorganised sector. This includes workers in the organised sector who are not members of ESIC/EPFO or not Govt. employees.

Registration Requirements

  • Aadhaar Number
  • Mobile number linked with Aadhaar.
  • Savings Bank Account number with IFSC code.
